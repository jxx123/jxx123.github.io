Apprentice: A World-Action Model with Calibrated Escalation

Two-level design: discrete latent-action decisions on a VLM backbone; continuous per-embodiment decode below.

architecture reference · every number extracted from source (cited inline as file:line) · repo: apprentice, branch dev

Abstract. Apprentice is a world-action model built so that a robot can be deployed before competence because it is calibrated: it knows what it does not know, and escalates ("show me") exactly at its knowledge frontier. The system is two-level. Above a hard interface line, all decisions are discrete tokens in one causal stream on a Qwen3-VL-4B backbone scripts/s4_cpt.py:79: Cosmos-tokenized video (64,000 codes tokenizers/cosmos_dv.py:18), binned proprioception (256 bins embodiment/normalize.py:55), latent actions from a Latent Action Model (12 live codes in 64 reserved slots configs/stages/s2_lam_v4_17a_fsq12.yaml:25, data/qwen_vocab.py:25), and in-grammar verdict tokens that make the policy its own judge. Below the line, small per-embodiment flow-matching heads decode each latent action into 4-step continuous action chunks models/lam.py:354. The same stream is simultaneously policy (z-spans), world model (future video tokens), and critic (verdicts) — so every deployment minute produces training data, and reinforcement learning stays entirely on the discrete side where likelihoods are exact docs/TECH_REPORT.md:147.

Figure 1: System overview. Left-to-right dataflow through both planes. Below the line, a frozen Cosmos DV4×8×8 tokenizer maps each 128×128 frame window to discrete indices (16×16 = 256 tokens per latent frame, vocabulary 64,000 cosmos_dv.py:8-18) and to continuous pre-quant latents (6,16,16) models/lam.py:11. The LAM’s video-only IDM compresses each latent transition (4 raw frames) into zₚᵣₑ ∈ ℝ¹⁶, quantized by an FSQ grid with levels [4,3] into one of 12 codes s2_lam_v4_17a_fsq12.yaml:25; the FDM reconstructs the next latent from (Lₜ, z), making z a bottleneck that must carry the dynamics. Above the line, dv, sv and z tokens join instruction text in a single stream consumed by the Qwen3-VL-4B backbone, which emits z-span plans, in-grammar verdicts, and future video tokens. Decode: planned z codes, fresh proprio state, and a visual conditioning vector are consumed by a per-embodiment rectified-flow head producing 4-action chunks models/lam.py:354-480. The dashed rust line is the reinforcement-learning boundary: exact-likelihood updates (GRPO) act only on categorical tokens above it; everything below trains by supervised flow matching or regression docs/TECH_REPORT.md:147.

Figure 2: One real serialized episode (template E). This 2-span record totals 827 vocabulary tokens (+ instruction text), of which 250 are supervised targets — measured on the rendered record. Supervision rules, as implemented: (i) dv/sv observation blocks after t=0 are CE targets for all episodes — the world model trains on failures too; motion weighting keeps CE on every dv token that changed since the previous latent frame and on a random 30% of static tokens (static_keep=0.3 e_serializer.py:30, commit b20987a) — otherwise ~90% of the gradient goes to background persistence. (ii) z-spans are targets iff the episode succeeded (filtered behavior cloning); on failures they flip to context so the policy never imitates failure while the world model still learns from it e_serializer.py:79. (iii) The Q-verdict — the episode’s eventual outcome token — sits directly after each z-span (p=0.25), training a fast evidence-free critic of the plan just emitted (commit 8303f8d); mid-episode <|verdict_ongoing|> judges are subsampled at p=0.25 after observations, and the terminal verdict is always present, upweighting decision-relevant endgame windows e_serializer.py:92-109. At inference the same grammar runs as a persistent-KV state machine: observation appends cost one forward (~280 tokens) whose logits give surprise and verdict health for free docs/E_STREAM_SPEC.md.

4The Latent Action Model in detail

IDM (inverse dynamics). Video-only by design: the encoder sees two consecutive Cosmos pre-quant latents (each 6×16×16, spanning 4 raw frames) and nothing else — no proprioception, no raw actions — so z stays embodiment-agnostic and human-video-compatible models/lam.py:1-13,174. Two drop-in architectures share the signature: a strided conv net, and a transformer treating the 256 spatial positions as tokens with a CLS pool (d_model 128, 4 layers, 4 heads lam.py:208-231). The state-free rule is explicit in code: “state NEVER enters IDM/FDM; it supervises z as a MASKED aux target (loss skipped where state doesn’t exist, i.e. human video)” lam.py:644-647.

Quantizer. Production (v4.17a) uses Finite Scalar Quantization: project zₚᵣₑ to 2 dims, tanh-bound, round to a [4,3] grid — 12 implicit codes, no learned codebook, no EMA, no collapse machinery lam.py:99-163. The earlier appendable VQ (EMA codebook with freeze-and-append) remains the default for v3 checkpoints lam.py:21-96. Either way the LAM is treated as a tokenizer: frozen and versioned before backbone training; changing it is a version bump implying a corpus rebuild lam.py:35-38.

FDM (forward dynamics). Reconstructs Lₜ₊₁ from (Lₜ, z) as a residual — the training scaffold that forces z to carry the transition, and the gate probe for verification; its predicted latents decode to pixels through the empirically recovered Cosmos quantization (EmpiricalFSQ, exact-match validated at fit time) lam.py:193-205,234-256 · fsq_calibration.py:1-20.

Aux heads (train-time only). Action grounding enters through per-embodiment heads whose gradients shape z but which are discarded at deployment — except the flow heads, which are the deployable decoder in v4+. The shared EE-pose head outputs a fixed (2 arms × 4 substeps × 10) tensor — 9-D SE(3) [6-D rotation + 3-D translation] + 1-D aperture — where every quantity is relative to the chunk start (the replan anchor) and scale-normalized (Δmidpoint/reach, Δaperture/max) lam.py:273-312 · ee_geometry.py:1-31. Measured consequences of the delta form: absolute keypoint targets were 100% linearly separable by embodiment (z learned robot identity, consensus probe 0.989); chunk-anchored deltas drop separability to 59.5% and cancel constant calibration offsets (0.21 mm residual vs 11.4 mm) lam.py:285-291, experiments/20260727. Absent arms are masked out of the loss, so a single-arm robot never teaches the head that the other arm is motionless lam.py:750-763.

Continuous decode. decode_actions maps planned code indices through the frozen codebook and samples the per-embodiment flow head: inputs z(16) ⊕ state(Dₛ) ⊕ visual cond(64) ⊕ previous action; 10 Euler steps; optional z-classifier-free guidance (v = v₀ + w·(v_z − v₀)); init=’zero’ integrates from the noise mean for a deterministic “mean sample” that kills cross-chunk mode switching — the safe deployment default lam.py:454-481,819-831. One z decodes to the embodiment’s native rate: 4 actions on franka (20 Hz), 6 on so101 (30 Hz) lam.py:293-295 · spec.py:147,177.

5Runtime: acting, judging, escalating

At inference the E-grammar becomes a state machine over a persistent KV cache: observe (Cosmos encode ~20 ms; append ~280 observation tokens in one obligatory forward, 30–70 ms) → plan (generate a z-span, ~35 ms/code) → execute the decoded chunk buffer open-loop. The obligatory forward’s logits are free telemetry: surprise (mean NLL of observed dv tokens = predicted-vs-observed disagreement) and the verdict readout. A speculative check forks the KV cache per chunk (~50 ms, discarded); surprise above threshold or a degrading judge triggers an event-driven replan — truncated spans are in-distribution by construction. Steady state: ~0.4 s of model time per 1.6 s of control docs/E_STREAM_SPEC.md. The verdict probability is the calibration signal that gates escalation — the “I don’t know → show me” loop in which human takeovers land exactly on the model’s own state distribution, and all learning updates keep exact likelihoods: GRPO over categorical z/EE/verdict tokens on the backbone, PPO over a small Gaussian residual on the frozen head, or supervised head refresh — never policy gradients through the flow head docs/TECH_REPORT.md:147-152.
